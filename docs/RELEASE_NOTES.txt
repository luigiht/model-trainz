RELEASE NOTES

Newest first. Each release has store-ready "What's new" text (Google Play allows 500 characters per language; the App Store 4,000) followed by fuller notes for reference.


1.1 (unreleased) - 4 November 2026

Not yet uploaded; app version numbers are unchanged until the next store build.

What's new (store text):

The line goes on forever! Ride through spring, autumn and winter, tunnel through hills, and meet new stations without end. Two new camera views: Chase, to orbit your train, and Cinematic, for film-style shots of the journey. A new upgrade, Longer Platforms, makes room for more passengers. Smoother train motion, too.

In this release:

- Endless line: station names never repeat; new towns are made from the old names.
- Seasons change as you travel: spring, autumn (orange trees, golden fields) and winter (snow on
  trees and land), then spring again. Sky, light and water change with them.
- Tunnels from station 10 on: stone portals and a lined tube through the hill.
- New camera views: Chase (drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, recentres when idle) and Cinematic
  (automatic lineside, orbit, tracking and aerial shots). The camera choice moved into Settings;
  V cycles all four views.
- New upgrade: Longer Platforms (+3 m of platform and +20% fares per level, up to 5).
- Smooth train motion at any frame rate (no more jitter while moving).


1.0 (build 1) - 29 October 2026

First release on Google Play and the App Store. Android versionName 1.0 / versionCode 1; iOS MARKETING_VERSION 1.0 / CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION 1.

What's new (store text):

All aboard! The first release of Model Trainz: lay track, run your little steam train from station to station and grow a cozy railway. Upgrade your shovels, hire a track gang, add carriages and save up for the Evergreen Express. Your game saves online with a Game ID, so you can continue on any device. No ads, no in-app purchases.

In this release:

- Lay track to reach each station and earn fares on arrival.
- Seven upgrades and two train sets at the Depot.
- Diorama and cab views.
- Online saves: continue on any device with your Game ID.
- Privacy policy and in-game "Delete this game".
- English, Spanish and Italian; reduced motion and keyboard controls.
