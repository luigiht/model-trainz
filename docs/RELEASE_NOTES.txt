1.0 (build 1) - 29 October 2026

First release on Google Play and the App Store. Android versionName 1.0 / versionCode 1; iOS MARKETING_VERSION 1.0 / CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION 1.

What's new (store text):

All aboard! The first release of Model Trainz: lay track, run your little steam train from station to station and grow a cozy railway. Upgrade your shovels, hire a track gang, add carriages and save up for the Evergreen Express. Your game saves online with a Game ID, so you can continue on any device. No ads, no in-app purchases.

In this release:

- Lay track to reach each station and earn fares on arrival.
- Seven upgrades and two train sets at the Depot.
- Diorama and cab views.
- Online saves: continue on any device with your Game ID.
- Privacy policy and in-game "Delete this game".
- English, Spanish and Italian; reduced motion and keyboard controls.
