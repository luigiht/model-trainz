## Full description

Your little steam train waits at the station. All it needs is track.

Model Trainz is a cozy, relaxing clicker game about building a railway across a hand-crafted, low-poly countryside. Tap to lay track in front of your train. When the line reaches the next station, all aboard! The train sets off, passengers pay their fares on arrival, and the next stretch of track is yours to build.

**BUILD YOUR RAILWAY**
• Tap, or hold, to lay track piece by piece
• Watch your train chuff from station to station through rolling hills and forests
• Earn fares at every stop and spend them on upgrades

**UPGRADE EVERYTHING**
• Bigger Shovels: lay more track with every tap
• Track Gang: a crew that lays track for you while you play
• Steel Rails, Fare Hikes and Extra Carriages: earn more from every journey
• Hotter Boiler: a faster train
• Golden Spikes: a lucky tap can pay out 20×

**COLLECT TRAINS**
Start with the Heritage Steam tank engine and its teak coaches, then save up for the Evergreen Express at the Depot.

**TWO WAYS TO WATCH**
Admire the whole diorama from above, or hop into the cab and look around from the driver's seat.

**PLAY ANYWHERE**
Your game saves online with a simple Game ID. Enter it on any device, phone, tablet or browser, and pick up right where you left off.

**A COZY GAME, NO STRINGS ATTACHED**
• No ads
• No in-app purchases: everything is earned by playing
• No accounts, no tracking
• Adjustable graphics quality for older devices
• Available in English, Spanish and Italian

Grab your shovel, the 9:15 won't build its own track.
